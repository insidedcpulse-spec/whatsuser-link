## ⚡ What is the WhatsApp Username Rollout?

Historically, WhatsApp required sharing a raw international phone number (e.g., `+1 415 555 2671`) to start a chat. In 2026, Meta introduced **@Usernames**, allowing users to pick a unique identifier—similar to Instagram or Telegram handles—that opens a direct WhatsApp chat without exposing their phone number.

The 2026 rollout is **phased by region and account type**:

1. **Beta Testers & Business Accounts**: Early access for WhatsApp Business Platform users and Meta Verified accounts.
2. **Global Consumer Rollout**: Region-by-region rollout for standard iOS and Android WhatsApp apps.

## 🛠️ How to Check Username Availability & Setup Rules

To ensure your preferred handle is accepted, WhatsApp enforces strict formatting criteria:

- **Length**: Must be between 3 and 35 characters.
- **Allowed Characters**: Lowercase letters (`a-z`), numbers (`0-9`), dots (`.`), and underscores (`_`).
- **Letter Requirement**: Must contain at least one lowercase letter.
- **Forbidden Prefixes & Suffixes**: Cannot start with `www.` or end with domain extensions like `.com`, `.net`, `.org`, or file extensions (`.png`, `.js`).

### What is the 4-Digit Username Key?

To prevent spam and unwanted discovery, WhatsApp introduced an optional **Username Key** (a 4 to 8 digit PIN). When enabled, a person must know both your `@username` and your `Username Key` to start a chat with you.
