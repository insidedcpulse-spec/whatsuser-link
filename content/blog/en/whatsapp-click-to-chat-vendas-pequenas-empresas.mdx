**Short answer:** WhatsApp click-to-chat turns any link or QR code into a conversation starter. You put a `wa.me` link where customers already look (website, Instagram bio, flyers, product labels, email signature), pre-fill a message that says what they want, and reply from your phone. It costs nothing, needs no API, and works with the free WhatsApp Business app.

## Why click-to-chat works for small businesses

Customers prefer asking a quick question in a chat they already use over filling in a form. A click-to-chat link removes the steps between interest and conversation: no saving a number, no typing it, no new app. The visitor taps, sees your message ready to send, and presses send.

## Message templates that convert

Keep them short and specific so the customer only has to press send:

- **Quote request:** "Hi! I'd like a quote for [service]."
- **Booking:** "Hello, I'd like to book [service] for [day]."
- **Product question:** "Hi, is [product] available in size M?"
- **Support:** "Hi, I need help with order #…"

## Track which channel brings customers

You don't need analytics. Give each channel its own pre-filled message, or add a short tag:

- Website: `Hi! I came from your website`
- Flyer: `Hi! I came from your flyer`
- Instagram: `Hi! I came from Instagram`

## Use WhatsApp Business app features

The free WhatsApp Business app adds tools that matter for selling: a business profile, a product catalog, quick replies for common answers, labels to mark leads and customers, greeting and away messages, and a short link to your chat. Using them keeps conversations fast and organized without extra software.

## Respect opt-in and spam rules

Click-to-chat works because the customer starts the conversation. Do not message people who did not contact you or agree to hear from you, and do not build contact lists from scraped numbers. Follow WhatsApp's terms and local anti-spam and data-protection laws.

## A simple weekly routine

1. Place one link or QR per channel.
2. Answer new chats within the same business day.
3. Label leads (new, quote sent, customer).
4. Review which channel message appears most and put more effort there.

## FAQ

**Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?** No. The Business app and a wa.me link are enough to start.

**Is click-to-chat free?** Creating and using wa.me links is free.

**Can several people answer the same number?** The Business app supports linked devices, so staff can reply from different devices.

**Can I use my personal number?** You can, but a separate Business number keeps work and personal chats apart.
